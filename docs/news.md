## 2026

### November 4, 2026 — ALCF feature / infrastructure news

“New systems boost capabilities of the ALCF Inference Service” covers the expansion of the ALCF Inference Service with Tara and Minerva and features ChemGraph as an example of agentic scientific workflows using hosted language models. [ALCF article](https://www.alcf.anl.gov/news/new-systems-boost-capabilities-alcf-inference-service)

### October 29, 2026 — Conference presentation

Murat Keçeli presented “Agentic AI and Supercomputers: From Quantum Chemistry to Autonomous Discovery” at Future Labs Live USA, featuring ChemGraph, foundation models, and high-performance computing. [Conference program](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/future-labs-live-usa/speaker-murat-KE%C3%87ELI.stm)

### October 21–22, 2026 — OSI Hackathon / ChemGraphSafe

The ChemGraphSafe team participated in the Open Scientific Intelligence (OSI) Hackathon and developed a research prototype for screening potentially unsafe chemistry-related requests to scientific agents. The team evaluated decision models and LLMs using SciHazard and SciSafetyBench for request triage (ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK). The longer-term goal is to improve and integrate this safety layer into ChemGraph; it is not yet a production enforcement mechanism. [ChemGraphSafe repository](https://github.com/Autonomous-Scientific-Agents/ChemGraphSafe) · [Project website](https://autonomous-scientific-agents.github.io/ChemGraphSafe/) · [OSI Hackathon](https://llmhackathon.github.io/)

### September 22, 2026 — Peer-reviewed publication

“ChemGraph-XANES: an agentic framework for XANES simulation and curation” was published in *RSC Advances* **16**, 56195–56207. The study reports RAG-grounded parameter selection, FDMNES/Parsl execution, and reproducible spectroscopy data curation. [RSC Advances article](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ra06166d) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42775028/)

ChemGraph v0.7.0 was released on PyPI, with persistent interactive CLI sessions, durable agent workflows, and optional integrations for XANES, molecular docking, retrieval-augmented generation, and distributed execution. [Release notes](https://github.com/argonne-lcf/ChemGraph/releases/tag/v0.7.0) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/chemgraph/0.7.0/)
