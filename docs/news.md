### November 4, 2026 — ALCF feature / infrastructure news

### October 21–22, 2026 — OSI Hackathon / ChemGraphSafe

The ChemGraphSafe team participated in the Open Scientific Intelligence (OSI) Hackathon and developed a research prototype for screening potentially unsafe chemistry-related requests to scientific agents. The team evaluated decision models and LLMs using SciHazard and SciSafetyBench for request triage (ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK). The longer-term goal is to improve and integrate this safety layer into ChemGraph; it is not yet a production enforcement mechanism. [ChemGraphSafe repository](https://github.com/Autonomous-Scientific-Agents/ChemGraphSafe) · [Project website](https://autonomous-scientific-agents.github.io/ChemGraphSafe/) · [OSI Hackathon](https://llmhackathon.github.io/)

### September 22, 2026 — Peer-reviewed publication

ChemGraph-XANES: an agentic framework for XANES simulation and curation published in RSC Advances, 16, 56195–56207 (DOI: 10.1039/d6ra06166d). The study reports RAG-grounded parameter selection, FDMNES/Parsl execution, and reproducible spectroscopy data curation. [RSC Advances article](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ra06166d) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42775028/)
